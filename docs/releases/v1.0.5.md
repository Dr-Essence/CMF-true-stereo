### Earpiece leveller
A new, optional part of the stereo protection: it brings up the earpiece's **quiet and medium parts**, so the earpiece side keeps up with the speaker. Most noticeable with films, classical music and older recordings; loud modern pop is already levelled in the studio and changes little.

- **Nothing else changes:** loud parts, bass and every frequency stay as they are, and the earpiece's peaks never go above what it already gets today at full volume.
- **Silence stays silent:** the hiss between tracks is not raised.
- **Strengths:** Off (default), Light, Medium, Strong (at most 4 / 8 / 12 dB on the quietest parts).

Set it in **CMF Stereo** or **CMF Stereo Pro** (app v1.0.60+, in Stereo), or in the terminal: `su -c stereoctl protect leveller medium`.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and **reboot**: the new protection library loads after the reboot. Your settings are kept.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it); the module's own protection stands in. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
