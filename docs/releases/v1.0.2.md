### VLC follows the phone too
v1.0.1 let Android do the turn-the-phone swap, and the test app, music players and InShot followed the rotation. VLC did not.

Why, measured with the audio driver's own recordings: Android swaps left and right itself with the earpiece on your right, **except for an app that puts its own audio effect on its sound** (VLC does). That sound reaches the driver unswapped.

v1.0.2 swaps exactly those, and only those:
- While something plays with the earpiece on your right, the module's background service checks whether a playing app has its own effect, and tells the driver which output it plays on.
- The driver swaps only that output, only in that orientation. Everything Android already swaps is left alone.

Nothing to set up, no per-app list. It takes up to 2 seconds after you press play or turn the phone.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and **reboot**. Your settings are kept.

### Test it yourself
**LEFT_ONLY_3min.wav** in VLC, in Stereo: earpiece on your left, only the **earpiece** plays; turn the phone (earpiece on your right), wait 2 s, only the **speaker** plays.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
