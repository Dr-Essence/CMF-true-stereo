### Both speakers in games, not only in music players
In "While playing" mode (the default, and the only mode in the clean CMF Stereo app), games and most apps that are not music players played on the bottom speaker only. The earpiece joined in only with "Always on".

Why, measured on the phone: the module watched one switch inside the audio DSP, the one for its **music** task. Games play through the DSP's **fast** task, which has its own switch, so the module never saw them playing.

v1.0.3 watches every DSP playback task (music, fast, primary, deep buffer, VoIP, offload): any sound from any app now turns the earpiece on, and it goes off again when everything stops.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and **reboot**. Your settings are kept; this one is updated for you.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
