### What's new
- **Quieter kernel log.** The daemon checked the speaker amp's volume every 2 seconds, and the Awinic driver writes a kernel log line for every check, about 20 a minute. It now checks when playback starts and every 20 seconds. Stereo levels are held exactly as before.

Flash over v0.9.3 and reboot. Your settings are kept.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

- **Mono** (default): both speakers play the full mix, with Awinic speaker protection.
- **Stereo**: pick Stereo in the app (or `su -c stereoctl channels stereo`), reboot. Earpiece left, speaker right.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
