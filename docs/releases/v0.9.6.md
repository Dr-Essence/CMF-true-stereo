### What's new
- **Updates in Magisk.** From this version on, Magisk (and KernelSU / APatch) shows an **Update** button when a new release is out, and installs it straight from here. Install v0.9.6 by hand once; after that, updates come to you.
- **Checksums.** Every release lists the SHA-256 of each file (also in `SHA256SUMS.txt`), so you can check your download is the genuine file from this page.
- The module's description in Magisk is up to date (Stereo switches at once; left and right follow the screen).

Flash over v0.9.5 and reboot. Your settings are kept.

### Test it yourself
Two test files are attached to this release:
- **LEFT_ONLY_3min.wav**: sound in the left channel only. In Stereo, with the earpiece on your left, only the **earpiece** plays; turn the phone (earpiece on your right) and only the **speaker** plays. In Mono both play.
- **LEFT10s_RIGHT10s.wav**: a voice says "left", then 10 s of sound from the left side only; then "right" and the same from the right side. Six rounds.

Play them with any music app (VLC, USB Audio Player Pro, Files...), with auto-rotate on.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

- **Mono** (default): both speakers play the full mix, with Awinic speaker protection.
- **Stereo**: pick Stereo in the app (or `su -c stereoctl channels stereo`). Applied at once. Earpiece left, speaker right; swapped when turned the other way.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
