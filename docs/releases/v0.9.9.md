### What's fixed
- **Action button → Stereo crashed the sound.** Switching to Stereo from Magisk's Action button loaded an empty file in place of the audio driver, and Android's audio service kept crashing (no sound, high CPU) until a reboot. The button now works, and if a switch ever fails, the module puts the original files back before restarting the audio driver — never empty ones.
- **Left and right reversed in some apps when turned.** Nothing's Dirac tuning picks a mode for each app: **MUSIC** for most music players (it then swaps left and right with the rotation itself), **MOVIE** for video apps like VLC (it does not). The module used to guess by output, which went wrong once USB Audio Player Pro played through the same output as VLC. It now reads Dirac's mode and swaps only when Dirac does not.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and reboot. Your settings are kept.

### Please check
Play **LEFT_ONLY_3min.wav** in USB Audio Player Pro (Android driver) and in VLC, with the earpiece on your left and then on your right: the left channel should always come from the side that is on your left.

### Test it yourself
Two test files are attached to this release:
- **LEFT_ONLY_3min.wav**: sound in the left channel only. In Stereo, with the earpiece on your left, only the **earpiece** plays; turn the phone (earpiece on your right) and only the **speaker** plays. In Mono both play.
- **LEFT10s_RIGHT10s.wav**: a voice says "left", then 10 s of sound from the left side only; then "right" and the same from the right side. Six rounds.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

- **Mono** (default): both speakers play the full mix, with Awinic speaker protection.
- **Stereo**: pick Stereo in the app, tap **Action** in Magisk, or run `su -c stereoctl channels stereo`. Applied at once. Earpiece left, speaker right; swapped when turned the other way.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.
