### Stereo protection, built from measurements
The CMF L/R Test's DRIVER STUDY measured both speakers, and what Awinic's protection does in Mono. Two things stood out in Stereo: the bottom speaker got about **10 dB more deep bass** than Awinic ever lets through, and the earpiece **clipped** in its loudest 1.5 dB.

v1.0.4 adds the module's own protection, running inside the audio driver for every app:
- **Deep bass guard** (Off / Light / **Medium** / Strong): holds back only the deepest bass (below 100 Hz) on the speaker, and **only when it gets loud**. Quiet bass is untouched, unlike Awinic, which always cuts it.
- **Earpiece limiter** (on): stops the earpiece clipping at its very loudest. Its bass is kept.
- **Speaker boost** (0–6 dB, starts at 0): a louder speaker, with a limiter that keeps its loudest moments where Awinic keeps them in Mono. At 0 the speaker sounds as before.

Set them in **CMF Stereo** (v1.0.56+), **CMF Stereo Pro**, or the terminal: `su -c stereoctl protect`.

A safety net: if the audio service ever restarted 3 times within a minute in Stereo, the module puts the stock files back (Mono) by itself.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and **reboot**: the protection starts after the reboot. Your settings are kept.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it); the module's own protection stands in. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
