### The one swap for every app
Left and right now follow the phone the same way in **every** app, whatever it is — music players, VLC, YouTube, games, video editors.

What was wrong: Android's own audio mixer on this phone has a built-in speaker swap for when the phone is turned (made for Nothing phones with two speakers). It reached some apps and not others, so the module's swap either doubled it (sound stayed on the earpiece) or was the only one, depending on the app. Earlier versions tried to guess which apps it had already swapped; every guess held only for the apps it was tested with.

Now, while Stereo is on, the module switches that one piece of the mixer off (a single branch in `libaudioflinger.so`, only on the exact Nothing OS build it was checked against), and does every swap itself.

Update from Magisk's **Update** button and **reboot**. Your settings are kept.

### Please check
Use the **CMF L/R Test** app (`CMF-LR-test-….apk` from the [app's latest build](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/tag/latest)): all four buttons — MUSIC, MOVIE, GAME, SPEECH — should play the beep from the side on your left, with the earpiece on your left and on your right.

### Test it yourself
Two test files are attached to this release:
- **LEFT_ONLY_3min.wav**: sound in the left channel only. In Stereo, with the earpiece on your left, only the **earpiece** plays; turn the phone (earpiece on your right) and only the **speaker** plays. In Mono both play.
- **LEFT10s_RIGHT10s.wav**: a voice says "left", then 10 s of sound from the left side only; then "right" and the same from the right side. Six rounds.

### Install
Flash in Magisk / KernelSU / APatch, reboot. CMF Phone 1 (A015) only; on other phones it installs and does nothing.

- **Mono** (default): both speakers play the full mix, with Awinic speaker protection. Stock audio files, untouched.
- **Stereo**: pick Stereo in the app, tap **Action** in Magisk, or run `su -c stereoctl channels stereo`. Applied at once (the sound restarts; press play again). Earpiece left, speaker right; swapped when turned the other way.

> **Before you use stereo:** stereo turns off Awinic's speaker protection for media (calls keep it), so the speaker is held 6 dB below full output at level 10, 2 dB less per level down. Use Mono for loud listening. If the phone fails to boot twice with stereo on, the module switches itself back to Mono.

Companion app: [CMF-true-stereo-manager](https://github.com/Dr-Essence/CMF-true-stereo-manager/releases/latest)
